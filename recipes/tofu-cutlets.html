Makes 8 cutlets

Ingredients

  • Superfirm tofu - 1 block
  • Breadcrumbs - as needed
  • Flour - as needed
  • Almond milk - as needed
  • Italian seasoning - as needed
  • Marinara sauce - as needed
  • Shredded mozzarella - as needed

Cookware

  • Cookie sheet - 1

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Cut the tofu into slices.
  3. Put breadcumbs, flour, and almond milk on three separate plates.
  4. Add italian seasoning to the breadcrumbs.
  5. Coat each tofu slice in flour, then almond milk, then breadcrumbs.
  6. Put the breaded tofu slices on a cookie sheet.
  7. Put cookie sheet in oven, cook for 10 minutes.
  8. Take out breaded tofu slices, flip, and cook for another 10 minutes.
  9. Take out breaded tofu slices, add marinara sauce and shredded mozzarella on top.
  10. Broil for 5 minutes.
