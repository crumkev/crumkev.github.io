  • Basil Pasta

Makes 8 servings

Ingredients

  • Pasta - 1 lb box
  • Coconut milk - 1 15 oz can
  • Basil - ¼ cup (packed)
  • Onion - 1
  • Garlic - 3 cloves
  • Broccoli - 2 heads
  • Pepper - to taste

Cookware

  • Blender - 1
  • Large pot - 1
  • Fying pan - 1

Instructions

  1. Start boiling water for pasta in large pot, add pasta when boiling.
  2. Pick/wash basil, dice onions, mince garlic, and chop broccoli.
  3. Blend coconut milk and basil.
  4. Saute onion in frying pan on medium high heat for a few minutes.
  5. Add garlic and broccoli to frying pan, saute for a few more minutes.
  6. Add contents of blender to frying pan and cook down for a few minutes.
  7. Strain pasta and toss to coat in frying pan.
