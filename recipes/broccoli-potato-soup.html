  • Broccoli Potato Soup

Makes 4 servings

Ingredients

  • Gold potatoes - 1½ lbs
  • Broccoli florets - 4 cups
  • Onion - 1 (medium)
  • Garlic - 3 cloves
  • Veggie Bouillon - 1 tbsp
  • Almond milk (unsweetened) - ½ cups
  • Nutritional yeast - ¼ cups
  • Lemon juice - 1 tsp
  • Dried thyme - ½ tsp
  • Salt - ½ tsp
  • Ground black pepper - ¼ tsp
  • Olive oil - 2 tbsp
  • Water - 3 cups

Cookware

  • Immersion blender - 1
  • Large pot - 1

Instructions

  1. Dice the onion, dice the potatoes, and mince the garlic.
  2. Saute the onion in olive oil for 4-5 minutes on medium high heat.
  3. Add garlic, continue sauteing for 30 seconds.
  4. Add potatoes veggie bouillon, water, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  5. Simmer for 12-15 minutes.
  6. Add broccoli and simmer for another 7-10 minutes.
  7. Stir in almond milk and nutritional yeast.
  8. Turn off heat and use immersion blender to reach desired texture.
  9. Add lemon juice and adjust seasoning to taste.
