Makes 2 drinks

Ingredients

  • Frozen watermelon (cubed) - 2 cups
  • Tequila - 2 shots
  • Triple Sec - 1 shot
  • Lime - 1

Cookware

  • Vitamix - 1

Instructions

  1. Add frozen watermelon, tequila, and triple sec to Vitamix container.
  2. Squeeze lime into Vitamix container.
  3. Blend until smooth.
